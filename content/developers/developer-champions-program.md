# Developer Champions Program

Bioconductor is a big, diverse project that relies on contributions from
volunteers. The Champions Program is an effort to recognize individuals
outside the core team whose contributions ensure that Bioconductor
remains a healthy, welcoming, and innovative scientific community.

## 2026 Bioconductor Developer Champions

*Jenny Drnevich* Coming Soon!

*Kozo Nishida* Kozo Nishida is a member of [the Bioconductor Community
Advisory
Board](https://bioconductor.org/about/community-advisory-board/) and has
been actively involved in community outreach, training, and
internationalization efforts. He has contributed to [multilingual
resources and community
infrastructure](https://github.com/bioconductor-translations), and has
helped organize and promote Bioconductor activities in Japan and across
Asia, including [BioC
Asia](https://blog.bioconductor.org/posts/2021-11-11-biocasia2021/) and
local workshops
([Osaka](https://bioconductor-translations.github.io/2025-08-05-osaka/),
[Kyoto](https://bioconductor-translations.github.io/2025-09-11-kyoto/))

*Dario Righelli* Dario Righelli is a developer of
[SpatialExperiment](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/SpatialExperiment.html),
a Bioconductor package providing core data structures for spatially
resolved transcriptomics data. He also co-leads the [Bioconductor
Classes and Methods Recommendations Working
Group](https://github.com/Bioconductor/BiocClassesWorkingGroup),
contributing to discussions around object-oriented infrastructure and
practices within the project. His involvement in Bioconductor also
extends to community initiatives, including meetups and other activities
that foster exchange and collaboration among Bioconductor users and
developers.

*Kevin Rue-Albrecht* Coming soon!

*Claire Rioualen* Coming Soon!

*Charlotte Soneson* Charlotte is co-chair of the [Bioconductor Training
Committee](https://training.bioconductor.org/), one of the deputy chairs
of the [European Bioconductor
Society](https://bioconductor.org/about/european-bioconductor-society/),
current member of the European and North American Bioconductor
conference committees and the workflow working group, and a previous
member of the Bioconductor Technical Advisory Board and Code of Conduct
committee.
